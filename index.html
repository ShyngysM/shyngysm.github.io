Prague City Guide (Nov 17 – 19)

Historic center sightseeing, walkable nightlife, and traditional Bohemian dining.

Your Stay

Hotel Benedict Old Town Prague (Rybná 685/16)

Located right in the historic center near Revoluční street, within walking distance of Old Town Square, Dlouhá street nightlife, and the Vltava River.

🏰 Must-See Attractions

  • Old Town Square & Astronomical Clock: The beating heart of medieval Prague.
  • Charles Bridge: Historic pedestrian stone bridge lined with baroque statues.
  • Prague Castle & St. Vitus Cathedral: The largest ancient castle complex in the world.
  • Powder Tower: Stunning Gothic gateway marking the royal route.

🍽️ Must-Eat & Drink

  • Lokál Dlouháá: Freshly pumped Pilsner Urquell paired with traditional Czech pork roast and dumplings.
  • Café Imperial: Stunning art nouveau interior serving classic Czech beef goulash and svíčková.
  • Kantýna: Butcher-style Czech beef tartare and grilled steaks.

🥂 Walkable Nightlife

Buddha-Bar Prague

Sultry underground lounge, world music DJs & signature cocktails (3 min walk).

Duplex Rooftop Club

Famous rooftop club overlooking Wenceslas Square.

Hemingway Bar

Exceptional craft speakeasy in Old Town.

Kutná Hora Day Trip (Nov 18 Morning)

Sedlec Ossuary (Bone Church), Gothic wonders, and medieval tavern lunch.

Sedlec Ossuary Bone Chalice

🦴 Sedlec Ossuary (The Bone Church)

A Roman Catholic chapel adorned with the artistic skeletal remains of 40,000 to 70,000 people, featuring the iconic bone chandelier.

⛪ St. Barbara’s Cathedral

A breathtaking Gothic masterpiece dedicated to the patron saint of miners, featuring tent-like triple roofs.

🍽️ Must-Eat Food

Restaurace Dačický: Atmospheric medieval tavern serving hearty Czech roasted duck, game specialties, and dark Czech beer.

🎯 Prague Afternoon Fun (Post-Kutná Hora)

  • • The Chamber Escape Rooms: Immersive live escape games.
  • • Praga Arena Go-Karts: Indoor high-speed group racing.

Karlovy Vary Thermal Spa & City (Nov 19 – 20)

Luxury spa resort, indoor tennis, city bike tour, and healing mineral springs.

Your Stay

Spa Hotel Imperial (Libušina 1212/18)

Perched high above the city with sweeping valley views, featuring indoor tennis courts, fitness facilities, and direct private funicular access down to the colonnades.

🎾 Morning Active Options (10:00 - 12:30)

  • Option A (Hotel Tennis): Play doubles on Spa Hotel Imperial's indoor courts or workout at the fitness center.
  • Option B (City Bike Ride): Cycle along the Ohře River path past the Vřídlo Geyser, Mill Colonnade, Grandhotel Pupp, and Dvořák Gardens.

♨️ Afternoon Spa & Must-Eat

  • Imperial Spa Center: Mineral pools, saunas, and traditional balneotherapy procedures.
  • Spa Wafers (Lázeňské oplatky): Traditional sweet thin wafers baked with hazelnut or chocolate filling.
  • Grandhotel Pupp Restaurant: Fine dining and traditional Bohemian cuisine.
